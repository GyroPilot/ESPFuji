ESPFuji Installer

Temporarily withdrawn, 21 November 2026

ESPFuji has been taken down while we confirm a bug. The installer is switched off until a fixed version is ready.

What we found

While ESPFuji's Wi-Fi is connecting, or retrying a network it cannot reach, the ESProFile can occasionally store a block written by the Lisa with one or a few bytes missing. The Lisa is not told, so the damage shows up later. On one Lisa 2/10 it made the startup disk unusable ("You have no environments on the startup disk").

We reproduced it on a test bench with ESPFuji 1.1.1 and 1.1.2. The same test with Alex's emulator code and no Wi-Fi showed no damage in about 63,000 writes. Earlier ESPFuji versions have not been tested yet.

What to do

  • Back up your SD card (copy the disk image to a computer).
  • For any disk you care about, go back to Alex's stock ESProFile firmware (ESProFile repository) until a fixed ESPFuji is out.
  • If you keep using ESPFuji meanwhile, keep backups and avoid writing important work to that disk.

Updates will be posted here and on the ESPFuji Issues page. Please report anything you have seen there, never to Alex.

ESPFuji is Tim O'Connor's fork of Alex Anderson-McLeod's ESProFile firmware. It is not the official ESProFile firmware, and this bug is not in Alex's firmware. Alex has approved of ESPFuji being published but does not support it. The ESProFile boards and the stock firmware are Alex's.
